## 1. The EGM2008 "realisation" constant of −0.41 m (SI p. 14, "[TODO: cite]"; R2.M3(c))

**What it is.** NGA's EGM2008 geoid heights relative to WGS 84 are not the plain spherical-harmonic geoid of the EGM2008 coefficients. GeographicLib reproduces NGA's own synthesis program (`hsynth_WGS84`), and in doing so it adds a constant of −0.41 m. PROJ's EGM2008 grid (`us_nga_egm08_25.tif`) was built with GeographicLib, and ETOPO 2022's geoid layer reproduces it. ICGEM's evaluation of the same coefficients applies only the GM part of the zero-degree term (about −0.005 m), never a W0 part. Measured over our box, NGA/PROJ minus ICGEM is −0.405 m with an SD of 1.7 mm (`datum.nga_offset_m`; `results/provenance/geoid_difference.json`).
