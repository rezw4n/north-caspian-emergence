\subsection*{Products and sectors}
After the best-fitting vertical constant (the shape variant, which decides), \metric{erl.text.h1.majority_products} mispredict in a majority of the seven sectors (\metric{erl.text.h1.majority.n_sectors_min} or \metric{erl.text.h1.majority.n_sectors_max}); \metric{erl.text.h1.other_products} mispredicts in \metric{h1.mispredicts.shape.etopo2022.n_sectors}. On the documented datum chain without a constant (the total variant), every grid mispredicts in \metric{erl.text.h1.total.n_sectors_min} of the 7 sectors, and the median domain area biases are \metric{erl.text.h1.area_bias_total_min_pct}--\metric{erl.text.h1.area_bias_total_max_pct}\%: the vertical mismatch with the altimetric reference is common to all grids (constants \metric{erl.text.h1.shape_offset_min_m} to \metric{erl.text.h1.shape_offset_max_m}~m), and the total variant inherits it. The sector tables (tables~\ref{tab:ed_h1_sectors_shape} and \ref{tab:ed_h1_sectors_total}) give, per grid and sector, the levels whose median displacement exceeds the noise floor, out of the evaluable levels, and the median displacement; the block maps are figures~\ref{fig:ed_h1blocks_shape} and \ref{fig:ed_h1blocks_total}.

\subsection*{Deltaic and vegetated sectors}
The one-sided block permutation test compares the median block displacement in the deltaic or vegetated sectors (west, Volga, Ural) with the rest (table~\ref{tab:si_blocktest}). It is significant at $\alpha = \metric{params.alpha}$ for \metric{erl.text.h1.block.shape.significant_products} in the shape variant, and for \metric{erl.text.h1.block.total.significant_products} in the total variant; it is reversed for ETOPO 2022, whose deltas are filled from FABDEM.

\emph{Defect found after the first results, and both runs.} After the first hindcast, undefined distances were found encoded as the smallest 32-bit integer in the block table that feeds this test, which pulled down the medians of \metric{h1.block_h1.rows_changed_by_fix} blocks. The defect was fixed and tested, the hindcast rerun, and every table compared: all hindcast tables except the block table are identical between the runs. Table~\ref{tab:si_blocktest} gives both runs' $p$-values; no significance changed at $\alpha = \metric{params.alpha}$, and the rerun is the one reported.
