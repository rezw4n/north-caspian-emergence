\section*{Data availability statement}
The data that support the findings of this study are openly available at \url{https://github.com/rezw4n/north-caspian-emergence}. They comprise the level-binned observed extents and waterlines of the North Caspian (1992--2026) and of the two arms of Great Salt Lake, the hindcast tables, the waterline elevation model with its documented accuracy, and every derived table behind the figures and numbers of this Letter. The input data are openly available from the providers cited in the Methods and the Supplementary Information: Landsat, the altimetry levels, the five bathymetry grids, ICESat-2, GEDI and Copernicus DEM heights, ERA5, OpenStreetMap (Open Database License), ESA WorldCover, the World Database on Protected Areas, and the USGS gauge levels and lidar for Great Salt Lake. Gauge levels of the Coordinating Committee on Hydrometeorology of the Caspian Sea (CASPCOM) were used to estimate the offset to the Baltic Height System. The catalogue states no licence, so only the offset, its uncertainty and the code that recomputes it from the catalogue are released\citep{caspcom2026}. Protected-area polygons are not redistributed, in line with the database's terms. Results derived from the Copernicus DEM were produced using Copernicus WorldDEM-30 \textcopyright{} DLR e.V. 2010--2014 and \textcopyright{} Airbus Defence and Space GmbH 2014--2018 provided under COPERNICUS by the European Union and ESA; all rights reserved.

\section*{Code availability}
All code that produces the results, figures, tables and every number in this Letter, from the raw inputs to the text, is openly available at \url{https://github.com/rezw4n/north-caspian-emergence} under the MIT licence; one command (\texttt{make all}) rebuilds it. The repository also holds the pre-registration with its dated amendments and the decision log.

\section*{Use of generative AI}
This study made extensive use of generative AI. Claude Code (Anthropic; model Claude Opus 5.5), an agent based on a large language model, worked under the authors' direction. It wrote and ran the analysis code, figure scripts and tests, searched for and screened the literature, and drafted the pre-registration, the decision log and the text of this Letter and its Supplementary Information. The authors set the questions, approved the pre-registration and every logged decision, and reviewed and approved the results and the text. Every number is generated by script from the results file, and every reference was read and checked against its DOI record, with an audit trail in the repository (see also Methods). The AI tool is not an author; the authors take full responsibility for the content.
