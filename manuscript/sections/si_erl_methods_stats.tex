\subsection*{Statistics and reproducibility}
The hypotheses, metrics, spatial blocking, conformal procedure and decision rules were registered in the project repository before any hindcast, topography-accuracy or projection metric was computed. Each later amendment is dated and states its reason and what had been seen when it was made; the registration and its amendments are part of the code release. The statistical procedures are: the moving-block bootstrap of the level-noise model; the cross-validated ridge regression of the setup model; the block bootstrap of the screening scatter (\metric{params.bootstrap_n} resamples of blocks; $n$ = sampled training blocks); the permutation test for spatial structure of the lidar residuals (\metric{params.structure_perm_n} permutations; $n$ = training-block points); the paired block bootstrap of the H2 comparison (\metric{params.bootstrap_n} resamples; $n$ = test blocks); the split-conformal coverage with its test-block bootstrap interval ($n$ = test blocks); the one-sided block permutation test of H1 (\metric{params.permutations_n} permutations; $n$ = blocks with at least \metric{params.h1_min_block_n} distances); and the split-conformal intervals of the emerged area (H3; $n$ = \metric{h3.n_scores.gebco2026.uncorrected} held-out hindcast units per product and surface). Tests are judged at $\alpha = \metric{params.alpha}$. The registered tests and comparisons are applied to every relief grid and sector and reported in full. The registered H1 block test carries no adjustment for multiple comparisons; after review it is also reported with Holm's adjustment over its ten tests and at sector level (section~\SIrevision). Not every choice preceded the data: the choices made after data or results had been seen (the ice-season rule, the deep-water rule, the block-size cap, the robustness verdict rule, the GLO-30 and GEDI checks, the exposure layers and the headline framing) are listed in section~\SIdeviations, and the analyses added after review, registered before they were computed (Amendment 18), are labelled exploratory in section~\SIrevision. Every random draw is seeded, by default with \metric{params.seed}. All numbers in this paper are generated by script from a single results file, and the full pipeline, from the raw inputs to the figures and this text, is rebuilt by one command (Data and Code availability statements of the article).
