\subsection*{The registered verdict and its flaw}
Under the registered rule, the correction is decision-relevant at \metric{h3.relevant_scenarios.etopo2022}~m for ETOPO 2022 (verdict ``\metric{h3.verdict.etopo2022}'') and at \metric{h3.relevant_scenarios.gebco2026}~m for GEBCO\_2026, GEBCO\_2022, ETOPO1 and SRTM15+ (``\metric{h3.verdict.gebco2026}''). The uncorrected grids give \metric{erl.text.h3.registered.1.unc_over_fused_min}--\metric{erl.text.h3.registered.1.unc_over_fused_max} times more emergence at \metric{h3.scenario.1_m}~m than the fused surfaces (table~\ref{tab:si_h3forms}; table~\ref{tab:h3} for exposure). That magnitude comes from the degenerate error model described in section~\SIredteam{} (R3), and the registered verdict is reported as registered, beside the mechanism, not as evidence that the corrected surface is right. Figure~\ref{fig:nextmetres} shows the registered result as drawn before the red team.

\subsection*{Sensitivity forms}
The registered \texttt{constant} form stays inside the fitted shifts by construction. With it, the correction is decision-relevant at \metric{h3.scenario.1_m}~m only for \metric{erl.text.h3.constant.relevant_at_1m_products}, whose emerged band is filled from two sources (FABDEM above, lake depths below) that need different shifts; for the other \metric{erl.text.h3.constant.not_relevant_1m.n_products} grids the fused \metric{h3.scenario.1_m}~m emergence lies within \metric{erl.text.h3.constant.not_relevant_1m.max_reldiff_pct}\% of the uncorrected one. Their remaining ``\metric{h3sens.constant.verdict.gebco2026}'' verdicts rest on \metric{h3sens.constant.relevant_scenarios.gebco2026}~m for GEBCO\_2026, \metric{h3sens.constant.relevant_scenarios.gebco2022}~m for GEBCO\_2022, \metric{h3sens.constant.relevant_scenarios.srtm15p}~m for SRTM15+ and \metric{h3sens.constant.relevant_scenarios.etopo1}~m for ETOPO1; at \metric{h3.scenario.0p5_m}~m the uncorrected estimates are small because of the whole-metre staircase described below.

\subsection*{Transfer}
The registered correction transfers between E1 and E2 but not to E3, the lowest levels and the closest analogue to projecting below 2026 (table~\ref{tab:si_transfer}): there, the corrected emergence error exceeds the uncorrected one for \metric{erl.text.h3.registered.e3_n_corrected_worse} of \metric{erl.text.h1.n_products} grids, and the median transferred shift misses by \metric{h3.transfer.gebco2026.E3.shift_error_median_m}~m for GEBCO\_2026. The rule judges decision relevance, not accuracy, so the verdict stands as registered; it cannot be read as a validated correction.

\subsection*{The whole-metre staircase}
GEBCO\_2026, GEBCO\_2022, ETOPO 2022 (whose emerging seabed below the land-data band comes from GEBCO lake depths), ETOPO1 and, for most of its cells in the band, SRTM15+ store Caspian depths in whole metres. Their uncorrected emergence therefore jumps when a whole-metre contour emerges, between the \metric{h3.scenario.0p5_m}~m and \metric{h3.scenario.1_m}~m declines: at \metric{h3.scenario.0p5_m}~m the five grids give \metric{erl.text.h3.uncorrected.0p5.min_km2}~\kmsq{} (\metric{erl.text.h3.uncorrected.0p5.min_product}; \metric{erl.text.conformal_pct}\% interval \metric{erl.text.h3.uncorrected.0p5.min_lo_km2}--\metric{erl.text.h3.uncorrected.0p5.min_hi_km2}) to \metric{erl.text.h3.uncorrected.0p5.max_km2}~\kmsq{} (\metric{erl.text.h3.uncorrected.0p5.max_product}; \metric{erl.text.h3.uncorrected.0p5.max_lo_km2}--\metric{erl.text.h3.uncorrected.0p5.max_hi_km2}), against \metric{erl.text.h3.uncorrected.1.min_km2}--\metric{erl.text.h3.uncorrected.1.max_km2}~\kmsq{} at \metric{h3.scenario.1_m}~m. This quantisation is a property of the grids as users apply them; it explains part of the decision relevance at \metric{h3.scenario.0p5_m} and \metric{h3.scenario.1_m}~m under both forms.
