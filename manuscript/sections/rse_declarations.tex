\section*{Data availability}
The code (MIT licence), the derived data behind every figure, table and number, and the pre-registration with its dated amendments are openly available at \url{https://github.com/rezw4n/north-caspian-emergence}; one command (\texttt{make all}) rebuilds every result from the raw inputs. The input data are openly available from the providers cited in Section~\ref{sec:data}. CASPCOM gauge levels, whose catalogue states no licence, were used only to estimate the offset to the Baltic Height System; we report derived statistics and do not redistribute the levels \citep{caspcom2026}. Protected-area polygons are not redistributed, in line with the database's terms. Results derived from the Copernicus DEM were produced using Copernicus WorldDEM-30 \textcopyright{} DLR e.V. 2010--2014 and \textcopyright{} Airbus Defence and Space GmbH 2014--2018 provided under COPERNICUS by the European Union and ESA; all rights reserved.
