% Supplementary Information of the article: robustness checks.
% Every number is a \metric{} macro.

Each check was run as a variant of the primary analysis and judged by a fixed rule. A variant is ``robust'' if no headline decision changes and no magnitude moves materially (the elevation model's NMAD or bias, on the same points, beyond the tolerances fixed in the rule); ``partly sensitive'' if a sector-level outcome or a magnitude changes but no headline decision; and ``sensitive'' if a headline decision of H1 or H2 changes, in either direction. A check takes the worst verdict of its variants. The rule was finalised after the tables of two variants (fixed MNDWI~\metric{params.robust_fixed_mndwi} and AWEI\textsubscript{sh}) had been seen; the magnitude criterion added then can only make verdicts stricter.

Several variants (checks 1, 2, 3, 7 and 9) were run only on a seeded sample of the \metric{params.block_size_km}~km test blocks (sample R), and they were judged on the waterline elevation model: on the same R points, with the primary datum constant and nothing refitted, the headline is whether the model's NMAD stays below every grid's. These variants test the observation pipeline through the elevation model, not through the hindcast audit; the audit was recomputed only for the variants of checks 6 and 10. No check covers the near-term analysis (H3), whose sensitivity is the red-team test R3 (section~\SIredteam).

\emph{Not run.} The total-variant hindcast with the geoid difference taken without the height offset of NGA's EGM2008 realisation, the audit sides of the nearest-neighbour grids and of the check~9 bounds (a full hindcast each), natural-neighbour interpolation, the November season (check~3) and the windows at the end of E1 were not run. The Earth Engine items need pipeline changes first; the others did not fit the time and computing budget.

\emph{Data quality.} The lon--lat edges of the export tiles bow in the equal-area grid, so \metric{qc.seam_gaps.unseen_km2}~\kmsq{} of the computation cover lies in neither neighbouring tile and is never observed; all of it lies within a few hundred metres of a tile seam. It is not fixed in this version.
